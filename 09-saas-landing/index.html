06 / A FEW LOOSE ENDS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little context before you connect the dots.

What counts as a run?+

A run is one complete attempt to execute a workflow, from its trigger to its final step. A flow with four steps still counts as one run. This page uses illustrative pricing to show how that model could work.

Do I need to know how to code?+

The concept is designed around a visual workflow builder: connect a trigger, add steps, and set your rules. The interactive previews here demonstrate that experience; a production workflow editor is outside this landing page.

Can a person approve a step?+

Yes, human approval is central to the Kivo concept. A workflow pauses at a checkpoint and moves forward after a decision. Try the sample approval card in the features section.

What happens when I use more runs?+

The estimator adds $6 per extra 1,000 runs on Start and $4 on Flow. Scale includes up to 50,000. Annual billing applies a 20% discount to the estimated total. Move the usage slider to compare the plans.

Is this a live product?+

Kivo is an original Motion Lab concept demo and a reusable SaaS landing page template. Companies, activity, and metrics are illustrative. No live integrations, payments, or accounts are connected.

What happens to the email I enter?+

It stays in this browser page and is never sent to a server or saved in local storage. The form validates your address and shows a demo success state. Refreshing the page clears it.
